😊 About me

My research interests lie in efficient and resource-aware machine learning: continual learning, knowledge distillation and model compression, and compact representation learning. I study how a model should spend a fixed budget of memory and computation, and whether it can make that decision for itself as its data changes.

I am a research assistant at the VinUni–Illinois Smart Health Center. In 2026 I was a visiting researcher with Prof. S. Kevin Zhou at USTC (MIRACLE), where the work below began. I finished a B.Sc. in Data Science at the University of Information Technology, VNU-HCM, as co-valedictorian.
